Daily Safety Focus

Expose Before You Bore

Paint and flags indicate an estimated horizontal location. They do not guarantee the exact depth or path of a buried facility.

Two-minute talk

Five points for the crew

  1. Confirm the MISS DIG ticket and legal start time before disturbing the ground.
  2. Review Positive Response and make sure every listed facility owner has responded.
  3. Walk the entire bore path and compare markings with visible field evidence.
  4. Use approved soft excavation inside the four-foot caution zone.
  5. Expose utility crossings deeply enough to verify the planned bore path before crossing.
Foreman’s 60-second pitch

Paint is a starting point, not proof of depth.

Before the drill crosses a utility, we must know exactly where that facility is located. If we cannot verify the crossing or the markings do not make sense, we stop and get the information corrected.
